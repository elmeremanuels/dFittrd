# Measuring a garment for Curtain: 5 numbers per size

For shop owners. Time: about 3 minutes per size. You need a flat table, a tape measure and the garment. Write centimetres.

Why we ask: your size chart tells us which **body** a size is made for. It does not tell us how big the **garment** is: how far the waistband stretches, how wide the hem swings. With these five numbers we can tell a shopper "the band stretches over your hips with 3 cm to spare" instead of guessing.

## How to lay the garment

1. Lay it flat on a table, front side up, closed (zip or buttons done up), not on a hanger and not on a mannequin.
2. Smooth it with your hands. Do not pull it, except in step B.
3. Front and back edges on top of each other, side seams at the outer edges.

Every width is measured **straight across, edge to edge**, and then **multiplied by 2**. Write down both: the flat width and the doubled number. (If the two do not match, we see it and ask.)

## The 5 measurements (skirt or trousers with a waistband)

| | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Waistband, relaxed | Across the top edge of the waistband, lying as it falls, not pulled. x 2. |
| **B** | Waistband, stretched | Same place. Pull the band until it stops stretching comfortably, as you would to step into it. Do not force it. x 2. If the band does not stretch, write the same number as A. |
| **C** | Hip | Lay the tape **20 cm below the top edge of the waistband**, parallel to the band, and measure straight across. x 2. Always 20 cm, for every size and every garment, so the sizes can be compared. Put a pin at 20 cm on each side first if that helps. |
| **D** | Hem | Across the bottom edge, with the hem spread flat. x 2. On a wide skirt the hem will not lie in a straight line: follow the hem edge with the tape from one side fold to the other. |
| **E** | Length | From the top edge of the waistband straight down to the hem, at the centre front. Not doubled. |

Also tell us, once per garment:

- **Does it have a zip or buttons, or do you pull it on?** (For a pull-on garment, B has to go over the hips.)
- **Is there a drawstring?**

For tops and dresses the idea is the same, with other places: chest straight across under the armholes (x 2), waist at the narrowest point or the waist seam (x 2), hem (x 2), and length from the highest point of the shoulder to the hem. Say where you measured.

## Which sizes

- **Best: every size.** It is 3 minutes each.
- **Minimum: three sizes: the smallest, the largest and one in the middle.** We estimate the sizes in between and label them "estimated" for the shopper. We can only estimate well *between* measured sizes, so the smallest and the largest matter most.
- Never fill in a size you did not measure. Leave it empty.

## Worked example: Crimson Wildflower Maxi Skirt

This is what the shop sent us (received 13 November 2026), exactly in this form:

| Size | A band relaxed | B band stretched | C hip | D hem | E length |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AU 8 | 33.5 flat → **67** | 47 flat → **94** | 56 flat → **112** | 115 flat → **230** | **103** |
| AU 10 | 35.5 flat → **71** | 50 flat → **100** | 61 flat → **122** | 124 flat → **248** | **103** |
| AU 12 | not measured | | | | |
| AU 14 | 40 flat → **80** | 55 flat → **110** | 63 flat → **126** | 130 flat → **260** | **104** |
| AU 16 | not measured | | | | |

(The flat widths here are the doubled numbers divided by 2; the doubled numbers are the shop's.)

What we learned from it:

- The band, stretched, is 94 cm in AU 8, and the size chart says AU 8 is for 94 cm hips. So the band only just goes over the hips the size is made for. A shopper whose hips are larger than the chart says has to go up a size, even if her waist fits. We could not have known that from the size chart.
- The hem is 2.3 to 2.6 metres around: a real, wide A-line. Our earlier guess was 2 metres.
- AU 12 and AU 16 were not measured. We show them as "estimated". AU 16 is the largest size, so its estimate is the weakest: please measure it.

What we still need to ask for this skirt:

1. **At what distance below the band was the hip (C) measured?** The hip numbers jump unevenly (112, 122, 126). On a skirt that gets wider towards the hem, measuring 3 cm lower gives 4 to 5 cm more. Please measure C again at exactly 20 cm below the top of the band, on all sizes.
2. **AU 16 and AU 12**, all five numbers.
3. **Pull-on or zip?** And is the drawstring in the photos real (does it tighten the waist)?
