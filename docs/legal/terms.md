## Part A: everyone

### 1. What Curtain is

Curtain recommends a clothing size. It compares the size chart a shop provides for a product with the body details a shopper enters (or estimates from an optional camera scan that runs on the shopper's own device) and shows the size that is likely to fit best, how each size fits at points such as bust, waist and hips, and how confident the recommendation is.

### 2. Recommendations are estimates

A size recommendation, a fit description and any picture Curtain shows are **estimates**. They depend on the size chart the shop provided, the details the shopper entered and on modelling that cannot be exact. Curtain does not guarantee that a recommended size will fit, and pictures are not photographs of a real person wearing the garment. The shopper chooses the size and buys from the shop, not from us.
