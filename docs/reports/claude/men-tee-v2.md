# Men's tee v2: chest ease, shoulder strain, shoulder peaks, too-small sizes, UVs and print

Branch `claude/men-tee-v2`, from `claude/garment-engine`, 19 Nov 2026. This continues the M3 tee from tape (`docs/reports/claude/men-m3-tee.md`) and works on its defects 2 to 5. Every garment below is the **SYNTHETIC men's test tee** (`SYNTHETIC_MENS_TEE`, "not a product"). Its measurements are invented and its print is generated by code. Every picture is an estimate, and the embed labels it so ("Estimate · not measured").

## Verdict

Four of the four defects are fixed and measured, each with a cost that is stated below. Nothing here was measured on a real tee (M3 defect 1 still stands).

| M3 defect | Before (M3) | After (v2) | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2. Chest girth lost in the drape | Chest-line row of cloth: hull 0.6 to 2.2 cm under the tape, M3's tape 1.8 to 4.7 cm under | Hull within 0.3 cm (−0.2 … +0.3); M3's tape 1.1 to 2.1 cm under | A girth loop on every torso row under the armpit (viewer) |
| 3. Shoulder-line strain, own size | 0 / 0 / 6.8 / 26.2 / 37.1 % (S … XXL) | 0 / 1.3 / 0.1 / 1.7 / 4.9 % | The tee rests where its yoke fits, lifted up to 3 cm (viewer) |
| 3. XXL body in a smaller size | 115 / 106 / 84 / 89 % (S … XL), stretched shirt drawn | Not draped, not drawn: "does not close at the chest", 25 / 17 / 9 / 1 cm | The fit engine's `open` loop state decides (viewer + embed) |
| 4. Peaks at the shoulder points | Shoulder seam rises 0.88 … 1.76 cm over the arm's closing cone | 0.05 … 0.54 cm | The yoke is smoothed and held off the body by its own ease (builder) |
| 5. Plain colour only | No UVs | Four pattern pieces flattened in cm, one 1024 px atlas, a CC0 synthetic print, flat-lay photo projection | `pomUv.ts`, `pomPrint.ts` (builder) |

What it costs:
- **The underarm takes the strain.** At XL and XXL the tee is lifted 2 and 2.75 cm onto the fuller neck. That brings the armhole, built 2 cm under the armpit, up against the arm. p99 edge strain at XL goes from 14.3 to 16.8 %. The underarm proxy shows 4.1 % at XL (M3: 0.2 %). No view sees it: 0 pixels in all four views at every size.
- **The neckline sits higher** on the XXL neck (2.75 cm), so the crew neck looks smaller in front. That is what a tee does on a neck fuller than its yoke, but the lift is an assumption [A], not a measurement.
- **The bundle grows** from 169 KB to 257 KB (base.glb 141 → 234 KB, 124 KB gzip). That is far inside the 3 MB import limit, and the demo seed's `importBundle` takes it.

## What changed

| Piece | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Yoke smoothing | `spikes/garment-engine/src/pomTee.ts` `smoothYoke` | From 3 cm above the armhole up (blended over 4 cm), 40 Laplacian steps. Each step pushes every vertex back out along the body's distance gradient to at least its own distance off the body (at most `capEaseCm`). The shoulder points, the HPS and every rim stay fixed. A crease moves out, and a ridge moves in until the body stops it. The seam no longer follows the arm's closing cone, which stands about 1 cm above the shoulder line 2 to 3 cm inside the shoulder point. That bump was the "peak". The sleeve's top line is then stretched along the arm (nothing at the joint, all of it at the hem) back to the tape's sleeve length. |
| Panel tags and seam metric | `pomTee.ts` | Every triangle carries its panel (`POM_PANELS`: front, back, sleeve_l, sleeve_r). The build returns the sleeve rings and the shoulder seams. `shoulderSeamBumpCm` walks each seam from the HPS out and returns how far it rises above the lowest point already passed. |
| UV layout | `spikes/garment-engine/src/pomUv.ts` | Seams cut the atlas. A vertex gets one render vertex per panel, plus one at each sleeve's underarm. Render vertices 0…n−1 are the welded vertices in order and the copies come after, so the viewer's weld by position gives back the same mesh (tested, including the triangle order). Each piece starts from an unrolled guess and is relaxed as-rigid-as-possible (shape matching, 600 iterations, rotations only). A plain edge-length relax folded about 10 % of each sleeve's triangles and is not used. Pattern coordinates are in cm: on the body pieces `u` is across, 0 on the centre line, and `v` is down from the HPS. On the sleeves `u` is round from the underarm and `v` is down from the armhole. The four pieces share one scale in a 1024 px atlas (8.7 px/cm). |
| Painting | `pomUv.ts` `paintAtlas`, `flatLayPainter`, `sampleFlatLay` | The atlas is painted per texel from pattern cm, with a 1.5 cm bleed. A flat-lay photo is placed by two numbers: px per cm (the photo's chest line in px ÷ the chest width on the tape) and the point of the centre line at HPS height. The front photo goes on the front piece and the back photo on the back. The sleeves take a repeating print, because a flat-lay photo does not show a sleeve flat. |
| Synthetic print | `spikes/garment-engine/src/pomPrint.ts` | A breton stripe: ink on cream, 1.2 cm wide, every 4.5 cm. Two synthetic flat-lay "photos" are drawn at 8 px/cm, with a coral sun on the wearer's left chest (front) and a coral tab under the neck (back). They go through the flat-lay projection, the same path a merchant's photo would take. Licence CC0-1.0, generated by code, no photo, no product, no third-party artwork. This is recorded in `qa.json` `texture` (with the layout, the piece sizes and the flattening distortion). |
| Bundle | `pomBundle.ts`, `bundle.ts` | `buildPomBundle(..., { texture: 'synthetic-print' \| 'plain' })` (default `plain`). `buildBundle('synthetic-men-tee')` builds it printed. A textured base.glb (`writeTexturedGlb`) carries the welded normals (no shading seam at a UV seam) and an embedded PNG. Builder version `pom-tee-0.2.0`. |
| Chest loops | `packages/garment-viewer/src/engine.ts` `sewnTorsoLoops`, `girthLoopsAt` | A sewn top only. Each torso row from the body's armpit down 14 cm becomes a girth loop (±0.5 cm thick), held at no less than its graded girth. 3 cm bands, as in the step-3 control, held only their widest row. 1.6 cm bands burst at XXL. |
| Seat | `engine.ts` `seatSewnTop` | A sewn top only. Before the pins are chosen, the garment is lifted by the least multiple of 0.25 cm, up to 3 cm, at which no yoke particle is more than 0.3 cm inside the drawn body. `FitInstance.seat` reports the lift. |
| Not drawn | `engine.ts` `sewnNotDrawn`, `mount.ts`, `apps/web/src/embed/Garment3D.tsx`, `strings/en.ts` | A sewn top in a size where a placed loop is `open` is not draped. That state comes from the fit engine's band `tight` with the body larger than the garment (rule 4: the fit engine decides, not the drape). The body is drawn alone, the canvas says `data-garment="not-drawn"`, and `GarmentView.source` gives `{ kind: 'device', notDrawn: { <size>: { anchor, bodyLargerCm } } }`. The embed says (catalogue string `view3d.notDrawn`): "Size S is not drawn: it does not close at the chest on a body like yours (the body is 25.0 cm larger than the garment, more than its fabric gives). The size advice says how it fits." Every bundle that is not sewn drapes and draws exactly as before. |
| Demo | `scripts/demo-men.mjs` | `--close` zooms in on the shoulders and chest (front and a quarter turn). `--off-size` makes the XXL body pick S and XL and checks the not-drawn note. |

Not touched: `packages/fit-engine`, `packages/mannequin` (`families.ts` included), `.github/`.

## Measured versus assumed

All numbers are from runs on this branch. `docs/reports/claude/men-tee-v2-metrics.json` holds the drape numbers after, and `docs/reports/claude/men-m3-tee-metrics.json` those before. Drapes use the viewer's own steps (`prepareFit` + `relax`) on the male table bodies.

**Chest after the drape** (defect 2), cm. The row is the graded mesh's torso row at the POM chest line, taken after the drape. That way a lifted tee is measured on its own chest line, not at a fixed height that would cut it lower. *Hull* is a tape laid round the outside, over the folds. *M3 tape* is the M3 report's tape, the median radius per angular bin, which drops into folds.

| Size | Tape | Hull before | Hull after | M3 tape before | M3 tape after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S | 98 | 97.4 | 97.8 | 96.2 | 96.7 |
| M | 106 | 104.7 | 105.9 | 103.6 | 104.9 |
| L | 114 | 112.3 | 114.0 | 111.1 | 112.8 |
| XL | 122 | 120.1 | 122.1 | 117.3 | 120.8 |
| XXL | 131 | 128.8 | 131.3 | 126.5 | 128.9 |
| M body in XXL | 131 | 124.1 | 131.2 | 120.9 | 130.1 |

Found on the way: the cloth's own length round the chest row was never lost (M body in M: 106.0 cm before and after the M3 drape). The cloth folds in under the arms, and both tapes read the folds. The loops keep the row's hull. The bin-median tape still reads 1.1 to 2.1 cm of folds under the arms.

**Shoulder line** (defect 3). This is the largest strain of the pinned cloth above the shoulder ring less 1 cm (`necklineStrain`).

| Body / size | Before | After | Seat lift (cm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| S / S | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| M / M | 0 | 1.3 % | 0 |
| L / L | 6.8 % | 0.1 % | 1.25 |
| XL / XL | 26.2 % | 1.7 % | 2 |
| XXL / XXL | 37.1 % | 4.9 % | 2.75 |
| M / XXL | 28.6 % | 0 | 0 |
| XXL / S, M, L, XL | 114.5 / 106.1 / 83.7 / 89.3 % | not drawn: chest 25 / 17 / 9 / 1 cm | — |

Why the yoke was strained: grading scales the shoulders by the shoulder-width ratio (XXL +6.8 % on the M base), but the male table's XXL neck is 16 % fuller than M's. The graded yoke lay inside the body round the neck, and the pin settle pushed it out and stretched it. After the yoke smoothing it was briefly 122 % at XXL, because the smoothed cloth sat closer to the neck. Two other fixes were tried and rejected. Pinning only the neckline band let the tee slide down: the cloth over the shoulders is not held by the torso collider. A lift cap of 1 cm left XXL at 18 %. A cap of 2 cm left it at 9.6 % with p99 18.8 %.

**Strain and show-through, own size, after** (before in brackets): p99 edge strain 11.9 / 12.1 / 11.7 / 16.8 / 14.8 % (11.5 / 12.3 / 12.8 / 14.3 / 14.9). Body pixels through the cloth in four views: 0 at every size (0 / 0 / 0 / 0 / 1). Underarm proxy `mid`: 0 / 0.1 / 0 / 4.1 / 2.6 % (0 / 0 / 3.0 / 0.2 / 1.0). The shoulder and hem proxies are 0 everywhere. Off size on the M body: S to XXL drape, with 0 pixels.

**Shoulder seam bump** (defect 4), each size built on its own table body, cm: before 0.88 / 1.13 / 1.21 / 1.48 / 1.76, after 0.05 / 0.14 / 0.22 / 0.40 / 0.54. What remains at XL and XXL is the body's own cone under 0.9 cm of ease. 80 or 150 smoothing steps give the same.

**The tape gate** (M3, ±0.5 cm), every input on every size on its own table body: max 0.11 / 0.11 / 0.10 / 0.11 / 0.49 cm. XXL's 0.49 is the hem, tighter than that body's hips, as in M3. Sleeve lengths are exact again after the stretch along the arm; before it, M was −0.38. The device grading of the base is within 0.46 cm (XXL hem), unchanged.

**UVs** (`qa.json` `texture`). Flat ÷ on-garment edge length, 1st / 50th / 99th percentile: front 0.95 / 1.00 / 1.10, back 0.95 / 1.00 / 1.14, each sleeve 0.86 / 1.00 / 1.29. No folded triangle in any piece. Pieces flat: front 55.1 × 68.5 cm, back 53.8 × 68.5 (the chest width on the tape is 53), sleeves 43.1 × 21.8. A print is therefore up to 10 to 14 % off its size on the body pieces locally, mostly at the shoulders, and up to 29 % on the sleeve cap. A flat-lay photo's front is 53 cm across where this piece is 55: about 4 % mis-scale towards the side seams.

**Bundle.** 257 KB in five files: base.glb 233,864 (124,102 gzip), anchors.json 17,670, qa.json 7,235, grading.json 4,015, material.json 760. 7,618 triangles and 3,909 welded vertices (4,077 render vertices). The atlas PNG is about 10 KB, because flat colours compress. Drape per size 0.55 to 0.74 s in node. UV layout 0.4 s at build time.

**Assumed [A], new on this branch.** The yoke smoothing's reach (3 cm above the armhole) and steps (40). The seat lift's step, cap and tolerance (0.25 / 3 / 0.3 cm). The loop depth (14 cm under the armpit). That a tee rides up on a fuller neck rather than stretching round it. That "too small" means the fit engine's `open` and nothing else. The print and both "photos" are synthetic.

**On the male shape v2 bodies** (merged from `claude/garment-engine`, `maleShape: 'v2'`, a preview, off by default). A merge check read the POM tee 133 cm (XL) and 158 cm (XXL) round the chest on v2, against 117.5 and 126.7 cm on v1. That reading is a **measuring artefact**. The fixed-height tape at the POM chest line runs through the underarm, where sleeve and torso cloth touch, and the slice merges them into one loop. The same tape reads 161 cm on v1 XXL once the tee is lifted. On the chest line's own row of cloth the drape is right on v2: hull 98.2 / 106.0 / 114.3 / 122.5 / 131.8 cm, tape 97.7 … 130.3 cm. 0 to 6 pixels show, and no particle is inside the body.

What is real on v2 is the **armholes**. v2 keeps the heights and arms but its upper torso is about 2.5 cm wider each side, so the v1-built cloth round the armholes is stretched. The shoulder line reads 0 / 1.9 / 10.6 / 17.1 / 20.4 % (v1: 0 … 4.9 %) and p99 13.1 to 14.2 %. The seat lift cannot help, because that push is sideways. I tried judging chains and grading on the v1 twin: no better for the POM tee (11 to 24 %) and worse for the M1 tee's underarm (L 3.0 → 5.3 %), so it is not kept. The fix is building the tee on v2. Built directly on v2 bodies, the builder misses its tape by up to 3.3 cm, which is not investigated here. These numbers are bounded in `male.test.ts` ("the POM tee on the male shape v2 bodies").

## Open issues

- Real-tee calibration (M3 defect 1): unchanged. One real tee taped flat, built, compared with its photos, and its flat-lay photo projected with a measured scale.
- The seat lift stands in for grading the yoke by the body. A device grading with a neck ring (the yoke grown with the neck, not the shoulder width) would let XL and XXL sit where they were built and spare the underarm. That needs a decision on how the tape's neck width grades.
- Underarm strain at XL (p99 16.8 %, proxy 4.1 %): the armhole drop (2 cm, assumed) is used up by the lift. A deeper armhole for large sizes, or a lift limited by the armpit, are the two options to measure.
- The not-drawn rule covers sewn tops only. Every other bundle still draws a stretched garment on a body it does not close on. The rule is general, but applying it changes the women's views, so it needs its own review.
- The print: stripes match neither across the armhole nor exactly at the side seams. A merchant photo needs its two calibration numbers (px per cm, HPS centre) from the import, which is not built. Photo projection for the sleeves is not done (they take the print or a colour).
- The flattening is not conformal at the sleeve cap (29 % at p99). For a print with text, a seam in the cap or a conformal parameterisation would be better.
- Male shape v2: build the tee on v2 (the builder misses its tape by up to 3.3 cm there; the armholes of the v1-built tee strain 11 to 20 % from L up on v2).
- `qa.json` `meshHealth.triangleBudget` says [8000, 15000] and the tee has 7,618 (as in M3).
