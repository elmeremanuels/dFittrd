# Merchant onboarding: from "install" to "Try it on with a good size" (branch `claude/merchant-onboarding`)

19 Nov 2026. I walked the path a brand-new pilot merchant takes, as Bohohippychic would: Australian boho women's
fashion, AU sizes 8 to 16, maxi skirts and dresses. On Shopify that is "installs the app"; on WooCommerce it is
"activates the plugin". The goal is the first product showing the "Try it on" button with good size advice. I
fixed the places where a non-technical merchant would get stuck. Screenshots are in
`docs/screens/claude/merchant-onboarding/` (phone 390 px and desktop 1280 px, deviceScaleFactor 2), with the
timing data in `report.json` there.

## Result in one paragraph

A new merchant now sees one "Get set up" list of five steps:
1. Connect
2. Add a size chart
3. Measure one skirt
4. Turn the button on
5. Check it on your phone

The same list appears in the Shopify admin home, in the merchant console and on the WooCommerce settings page.
Each step has a button to the right screen and ticks itself once its facts are true. In the timed Playwright walk
the five Shopify steps took **12.1 s** of machine time. The **estimated merchant time is 18.7 minutes from
install to a size on the phone**. 9 of those minutes are the tape measuring itself, so it is **9.7 minutes
without it**. Every check in the walk passed (`failures: []`).

## What I built

**One checklist, three surfaces** (`packages/schema/src/merchantSetup.ts`)
- `merchantSetupSteps(facts, platform)` is pure and returns the five steps in plain English, with platform
  variants. For example, Shopify assigns a chart "in one go" from the chart builder, while WooCommerce does it
  per product with "Start from a template". Each step has a title, a detail, an action target, a minutes hint
  and, for steps 4 and 5, a "tick by hand" label.
- How each step ticks itself:

  | Step | Done when |
  |---|---|
  | Connect | Always, once the api answers |
  | Size chart | At least 1 product has a chart and its type |
  | Measure | At least 1 product has shop-`measured` tape (estimates do not count) |
  | Button | A "Try it on" was opened in the last 30 days, or a size was shown, or the merchant ticked it |
  | Phone | A size was shown, or the merchant ticked it |

- `productNextStep()` replaces the raw field keys the products list used to show (`sizeChart, flatLayPhoto`)
  with one plain sentence and one button: "Add a size chart", "Measure it" or "Edit product".
  - Its badges are "Not ready yet", "Ready: better with measurements" and "Ready".
- `sizeAdviceScore()` (0–100) replaces the completeness percentage. The old score counted 3D-only fields
  (flat-lay, drape, sheen), so a skirt that gives a good size showed as 40 %. The new score is:
  - the three required fields: 60
  - the shop's own measurements: 30
  - the fabric: 10
- `MERCHANT_PLAN_TEXT` / `merchantPlanText()` hold the plan copy (US$8.99 a month, flat, nothing per try) and the
  custom-distribution line. Under custom distribution Shopify cannot bill, so the copy says Curtain arranges
  payment directly.
- They also hold **Leaving Curtain**: the uninstall steps and what happens to the data, per platform.
- `CATEGORY_TEXT` gives plain garment kinds ("Skirt or trousers").
- `GARMENT_TAPE_DIAGRAM_SVG` is a drawing of a skirt laid flat with A to E and the 20 cm hip line.
- New "What to measure" words were added to `GARMENT_TAPE_FORM_TEXT`, with their PHP twin.

**api**
- `GET /v1/merchant/setup` and `PUT /v1/merchant/setup` (`services/setup.ts`) return the facts above, plus:
  - the skirt to measure first;
  - a QR code (generated by the api's own `qrcode`, no third party) of the first ready product's https page.
- `GET /shopify/charts/onboarding` now returns the same five steps. Its stored state (`themeBlockConfirmed`,
  `tested`) ticks steps 4 and 5.
- `GET /v1/merchant/products` items gain `measured`, `next` and `adviceScore`. They are optional, so older
  clients are unaffected.
- Rule 4 holds: only counts and the shop's own product pages leave the api. A test asserts no device id,
  measurement or recommended size is in the answer.

**Shopify admin** (`packages/shopify-app/admin/index.html`)
- The checklist is a full-width card at the top, showing progress ("2 of 5 done"), the current step highlighted,
  minutes per step, and one button per step:
  - step 2 goes to the chart builder (AU preselected);
  - step 3 opens the first skirt's garment measurements and puts the cursor on "How does it close?";
  - step 4 is the theme editor deep link with `addAppBlockId`, plus "I have saved it";
  - step 5 shows the QR code, the link and "It works on my phone".
- **What to measure** sits next to the form (above it, folded, on a phone): the drawing, what you need, how to lay
  the skirt, the A–E lines and "start with three sizes". There is also a line of help under "How does it close?"
  and under the hip distance.
- The products list has "Kind", "Size advice" (badge plus score) and "What to do next" (one sentence plus one
  button).
  - "Add a size chart" pre-picks that product in "Assign to products".
  - "Measure it" opens its measurements.
- There is a "Button not showing?" link to the theme editor.
- Empty catalogue right after install: "We are bringing in your products…". The page looks again every 5 s for
  2 minutes, then says how to look again.
- Errors use plain words through `friendly()`:
  - no connection → "We could not reach Curtain…";
  - an expired session → "Reload the page";
  - Shopify being down → "Try again in a minute";
  - the page opened outside Shopify → "Open Curtain from your Shopify admin (Apps → Curtain)".
  - No HTTP codes, no `SHOPIFY_API_KEY/SECRET` and no "Missing ?shop= parameter" reach the merchant any more.
- The plan card reads "Your plan · Curtain · US$8.99 a month". The custom-distribution text says payment is
  arranged with Curtain, and a paused plan explains what shoppers see.
- The "Hero renders" stat and the ledger are explained in plain words. The stat is hidden while it is 0.

**Merchant console** (`apps/web/src/merchant/Setup.tsx`, `MerchantConsole.tsx`)
- The same checklist sits on the Overview until all steps are done, with the QR code and the hand ticks.
- Products show the same badges, score and next-step sentence, plus where to do the work ("In WordPress: open the
  product and scroll to the Curtain box…").
- There is an empty state for "no products yet".
- "Leaving Curtain" appears under Usage & plan.
- Login errors are plain words. A rejected key says what to do; a network failure no longer shows a raw message.

**WooCommerce plugin**
- `includes/class-setup.php` shows "Get set up" at the top of WooCommerce → Curtain. Steps 1–4 tick themselves
  from local data:
  - the connection;
  - products with `_curtain_size_chart`;
  - tape with a `measured` value;
  - the "Show button" setting.
- Step 5 has "It works on my phone" (`setup_phone_confirmed`, new admin-post handler).
- The box collapses to one line when everything is done.
- The PHP words are checked against the TypeScript catalogue by `merchantSetup.test.ts`.
- The meta box got the same "What to measure" panel, using `assets/admin/skirt-measure.svg`. A test keeps that
  file identical to `GARMENT_TAPE_DIAGRAM_SVG`.
- The `.pot` was regenerated.

**The timed walk** (`apps/api/src/scripts/onboarding-walk.ts`, `pnpm --filter @curtain/api walk:onboarding`). It
runs install → connect (token exchange, backfill job) → chart → measure → button → phone → uninstall, then the
console screens and two error states.

## Measured vs assumed

**Measured (real code paths in this repo):**
- the api (Fastify on PGlite) and every route the admin and the console call;
- the admin HTML and script as served by `GET /shopify/admin`;
- the backfill and sync jobs;
- the chart assign;
- the garment-tape check and save;
- the checklist;
- the SDK, the Dawn-style storefront button and the `/embed` fitting room on Vite. The phone gets "We recommend
  10", labelled an estimate (`05-phone-size.png`);
- uninstall: the shop is paused, its purge is due in 48 h and `POST /v1/sdk/sessions` gives 404.

**Stubbed:**
- Shopify itself, through the offline `FakeShopify` (Admin GraphQL, webhooks, bulk export);
- App Bridge (session tokens and the collection picker);
- the theme editor, which is not driven. Only its deep link is checked.

**Stand-in:** the QR code points to `https://bohohippychic.com.au/products/crimson-wildflower-maxi-skirt`. The
phone opened the same product on the local demo storefront.

**Not real:** no real Shopify store, no real WordPress run in this branch, and no real phone. The Woo screenshots
are rendered outside WordPress through the test shims (`e2e/setup-shots.mjs`).

**Assumed in the minutes.** The machine runs a step in seconds, so I modelled a person:
- 3 s per click, 0.4 s per character typed;
- reading at 180 words a minute (words counted on the screens the merchant reads);
- off-screen work:
  - install screens: 60 s
  - tape measuring, 3 sizes × 3 min (`docs/MERCHANT-MEASUREMENTS.md`): 540 s
  - theme editor: 120 s
  - picking up the phone: 30 s

## Where a merchant got stuck before, and what changed

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Five setup steps (template, assign, theme block, test), with no measuring step | The five steps of the brief. "Measure one skirt" opens the right product with help next to the form |
| "Connected. Public key pk_…", and "No products synced yet. Publish a product and give it Curtain fields in the product admin (Metafields → Curtain)" (wrong advice: the chart builder does it) | "Connected. 6 products brought in from your shop", and "We are bringing in your products…" while the import runs |
| Products list: "Completeness 40 %" and "Missing: sizeChart, flatLayPhoto, ease…" | "Ready: better with measurements · 60 / 100" plus one sentence and one button |
| Errors such as "HTTP 502", "Missing ?shop= parameter", "The Curtain API is not configured for Shopify yet (SHOPIFY_API_KEY/SECRET)" | Plain sentences that say what to do |
| "Try-on sessions this period", "Hero renders (30 d)", "One try-on session = one product resolved by one shopper with a fit result, counted once per intent" | "Sizes shown this month" and "Each bar is one day…". The renders stat is hidden while it is 0 |
| No word on leaving or on data deletion | "Leaving Curtain": the uninstall path and what is deleted, when and by whom |
| WooCommerce settings: no guidance after activation | "Get set up" box at the top, ticking itself |
| How-to lines folded away under "How to measure" | "What to measure" open beside the form, with a drawing, plus help under "closure" and "hip distance" |

## Open issues (not fixed here)

1. **A WooCommerce merchant cannot get into the merchant console on their own.** Connect stores the secret key
   encrypted and never shows it, and the console asks for that key. The login page now says "Ask Curtain support
   for your console key", but the real fix is a product decision: a one-time console link from the plugin, or
   showing the key once.
2. **WooCommerce Disconnect does not cancel billing and does not delete the shop on the api.** It only clears
   local settings. The "Leaving Curtain" copy says so honestly (email support to cancel or delete). A
   `DELETE /v1/merchant/shops/self` with a Stripe cancel would close this; it needs a decision on whether
   deletion is immediate or delayed.
3. **Step 4 in the console for WooCommerce** does not see the plugin's "Show button" setting. The plugin's own box
   does. It ticks itself in the console once a "Try it on" is opened, or by hand.
4. **Bulk chart assign exists only on Shopify.** A WooCommerce merchant applies the template per product. The
   step text says that, but with many products it is slow.
5. The checklist counts "a size was shown" from try sessions with `fitShownAt`, not only billed fit sessions. A
   merchant checking from a theme preview, or the second try of the day, still ticks step 5. Billing is
   unchanged.
6. The theme editor step is assumed at 2 minutes, and was not verified on a real store (runbook step 12, D7).
7. The QR code needs a published product with an https `productUrl`. A store that is still password-protected
   shows the link, but a phone sees the password page first. The step text does not mention this.
8. The completeness score (`score`) still exists in the api and is used elsewhere (the 3D path). The merchant
   screens now show `adviceScore` instead.
