# Merchant settings inventory (D24)

Date: 20 Nov 2026. Status: research, nothing built. Answers the founder's request under D24 (docs/DECISIONS.md:171-180): what can move into the merchant dashboard, what merchants enter, what we prefill, what the shopper fills, and what must stay ours.

How this was made: six areas of the code were read twice (fit engine, fabric, plugins, merchant console, embed popup, 3D garments). The second reader checked each claim against the file on disk. Nothing was run. Where a claim was only read and not tested, the text says so. Effort is a rough judgement: small = hours to a day, medium = days, large = a week or more.

Words used here:
- **Setting**: one choice per shop, made once in the dashboard.
- **Data**: a fact about one product, chart or fabric that the merchant enters.
- **Prefill**: a value we put in the form first. The merchant sees it and confirms it.
- **Provenance**: the note that says where a value came from (measured, supplier sheet, public page, our estimate).
- **Stretch**: how far the cloth gives, in percent. It is the only fabric value that changes the size.
- **Drape**: how stiff or fluid the cloth hangs. It only changes the picture.
- **Ease**: the room a garment is designed to have around the body, in cm.
- **Tape**: the shop's own measurements of the finished garment.

---

## 1. Summary in ten lines

1. Point 10 is confirmed and wider than reported: a fabric without stretch or drape makes the whole product refused, on WooCommerce **and** Shopify **and** the plain API.
2. The fix is one shop setting with two choices: "show size advice anyway, with a notice" (default) or "hold the product back until it is complete".
3. Do not throw the whole fabric away. Keep what the merchant entered, leave out only the missing part, assume 0 % stretch. This differs slightly from the wording of D24 point 4, so the founder must confirm (question 1).
4. There is no Settings page today, and a merchant cannot open the console without an e-mail to support. That page is the first thing to build.
5. We found 28 real shop settings. Almost all are about the shop's storefront: language, wording, colours, which features to offer. Only three touch size advice: incomplete data, the shop's audience, and what to do when no size fits.
6. Most of what looked like "settings" is really **data per product**. The work there is simpler questions: named choices ("a little stretch", "runs small") instead of numbers and free text.
7. Prefill is allowed for composition, drape, weight, chart templates, category and audience, always shown with its source. A prefilled stretch must not count until the merchant confirms it, because our guess would recommend a smaller size.
8. The fabric library of D24 point 3 does not exist yet. Start by saving each shop's own fabrics, private to that shop. Today's built-in library is generic estimates, not mill data.
9. About 48 things must stay fixed: what "tight" means, the confidence we show, the 0 % stretch fallback, the estimate label, privacy rules. A merchant setting there would let a shop make advice wrong without anyone seeing it.
10. Build order: (1) point 10 plus the Settings page; (2) simpler product forms plus "My fabrics"; (3) storefront settings and the 3D data forms.

Settings we were asked about and advise **not** to build:

- **Default stretch or default fabric for the shop.** A jersey default lands on the one woven shirt and advises it too tight, with nobody seeing it. Use the bulk action in section 3 (D21) instead. packages/schema/src/sizeAdvice.ts:24-29.
- **Go-live gate on the completeness score.** The stored status already comes from the three size-advice fields (apps/api/src/services/products.ts:52), not from the 0.5 threshold.
- **"Use estimated tape sizes: yes / no".** A size without tape values gets no pull-on check and competes as if it passes. Switching estimates off removes a safety check. packages/fit-engine/src/computeFit.ts:367, :439.
- **Pull-on allowance as a number, "my own fit preset", a strictness slider, a shop default fit preference.** Each moves every size and no small shop can judge it.
- **Shop ads opt-in in the console.** `settings.adsEnabled` is stored but nothing reads it. A switch without effect is worse than no switch. apps/api/src/routes/merchant.ts:64-65; apps/api/src/db/schema.ts:46.
- **API base URL as a visible setting.** Hide it behind a developer flag. packages/woocommerce-plugin/includes/class-settings.php:278-294.

### The fabric library (D24 point 3)

**What exists today.** No fabric table, no route, no picker. Fabric lives only inside each product. The built-in library in packages/schema/src/fabrics.ts (:23, :47-59, :157-211, :362; copy in packages/woocommerce-plugin/assets/fabric-library.json) has, by its own comments, 12 fibres and 38 constructions. Its sources are Wikipedia, Sew Guide, Spoonflower and two papers. Every stretch value is marked "Curtain estimate". **It is not mill or manufacturer data.** Prefill from factory websites, as the founder describes, is not built.

**Tables needed, in the smallest first step.**

| Table | Holds | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `shop_fabrics` (new) | One row per fabric a shop has saved: owner shop, name, composition (blend), the stretch answer and its number, drape word, weight, opacity, sheen. | Filled when a merchant saves a fabric on a product. Private to that shop. |
| a source per value, inside the same row | For each value: `measured` / `supplier sheet` / `public page` + URL / `from product text` / `Curtain estimate`. | Needed per value, not per fabric: one fabric mixes a published weight with an estimated stretch. A separate sources table can wait until a second data source exists. |
| public tier | Our library, versioned, read-only for merchants. | Stays in fabrics.ts at first. Every value a merchant sees shows its source and the word "estimate". |
| `products.payload` (exists) | Still gets a **copy** of the resolved fabric values, plus the id of the chosen fabric. | The fit engine on the phone keeps working without a lookup. |

A route lists a shop's own fabrics plus the public ones, nothing else. No shopper data ever goes in these tables.

**Editing a saved fabric** changes the advice of every product that uses it. Show the count before saving.

**What may be shared across shops.** Nothing yet (docs/DECISIONS.md:177). Our advice for the rule the founder still has to set:

- Everything a merchant typed is private by default. A supplier's spec sheet can be confidential.
- A fact from a public mill page may be shown to all shops as a fact with its URL. Do not copy whole pages (licences).
- Later, sharing can be an **opt-in per fabric, default off**. Never opt-out: a default that shares could leak supplier data without the merchant noticing.
- Do not add a "share my fabrics" switch before the rule exists. It would decide the open question by accident.

## 7. Proposed build order

### Slice 1: point 10, and a place to put settings

Goal: no product loses its size advice over one missing fabric number, and the merchant chooses the policy.

1. **Schema.** Make stretch and drape optional in the draft fabric. Keep every valid value that is present. Leave out a value that is present but invalid (stretch 150), with a notice.
2. **API.** In `upsertProduct`, apply the shop's choice (S1). Return a list of notices, reusing the existing `defaulted` list. Store a held-back product as "not ready, because…" instead of answering 400.
3. **Both builders.** The PHP twin and the TypeScript mapping follow the same rule. Remove the stars on stretch and drape (R2) and rewrite the help line (R3).
4. **Shopify.** Catch per product in sync and backfill (R1). Untick the estimated-stretch box (D18, F20).
5. **Settings page and the way in (S2)**, with this one setting on it, plus the existing brand colour, logo and shop name (A1 to A3).
6. **Notice.** In the product list for the merchant. For the shopper, a truthful line that fabric stretch was not taken into account. Whether the popup shows the `defaulted` notice today was not verified.
7. **Test first** with a fabric that has only a name, on WooCommerce, Shopify and the plain API. The failure was read from the code, not run.

Effort: medium. Depends on question 1.

### Slice 2: simpler product forms, and "My fabrics"

Goal: a small-shop owner can answer every question that changes a size without knowing a number.

1. Stretch as named choices; drape as four words; prefilled stretch counts only after confirmation (D9, D10, F20).
2. Runs small / true / large instead of parsed text (D7). Cut as a closed list (D5). Tick boxes for adjustable zones and exact-fit points (D15, D16). True-to-size score removed (F7).
3. Audience: shop question and per-product picker, with the guess shown (S3, D4). Category mapping table (D1).
4. "My fabrics": the `shop_fabrics` table with a source per value, a picker on the product, the bulk action (D21, D22). Private per shop.
5. Material attribute dropdown (S6); the score rewards a confirmed stretch (R6); one honesty rule for men and women (F17).
6. A fabric form for Shopify, which has none today.

Effort: large, mostly forms on two platforms.

### Slice 3: storefront settings and the 3D data

Goal: the shop's own voice and look, and the missing garment facts.

1. Storefront settings S7 to S22 and S26 to S28, cheapest first: the on/off switches, then units, shoe sizes, accent colour, shop-voice texts.
2. Language packs, Dutch first, when D21 allows (S4).
3. 3D: the shop switch once priced (S23), "See it on me" made reachable (S25), the 3D garment facts (D19), then the per-category measuring forms (D20), which are the largest piece.
4. Shared size charts in WooCommerce (D2). Delete-my-data and replace-key actions.
5. The remaining repairs R4, R5, R7 to R10.

Effort: large; can be cut into many small releases.

## 8. Open questions for the founder

1. **"Without the fabric" or "without the missing part"?** D24 point 4 says the product goes through without the fabric. All readers advise keeping what the merchant did enter (name, blend, a valid stretch) and leaving out only what is missing. Dropping everything would throw away a stretch the merchant typed, change the men's jeans rule unseen, and move the product to "unknown" in the fabric analytics. May we build it that way?
2. **Should "hold back" exist in the first version?** It costs the shop its size advice and helps few shops. D24 asks for the choice, so we propose to build it but keep it out of the first-run flow. Agreed?
3. **Sharing rule for the fabric library.** Our advice: private by default, public mill facts shown with a URL, later an opt-in per fabric that is off by default. Do you accept that as the rule, or do you want something else decided first?
4. **Prefill from mill and manufacturer websites.** This is not built; today's library is generic estimates. Do you want us to start collecting real mill data (which mills, and who checks the licences), or is "merchants fill it, we keep it" enough for now?
5. **Paused plan: show a note or hide the button, and what is the default?** Today the button stays with a "shop paused" note (D7). One reader would default to "hide". This changes D7.
6. **Language (D21).** When may Dutch ship, and who supplies the legal avatar texts in Dutch?
7. **The age range question.** It collects data nothing uses. Remove it, or keep it as a shop switch that is off by default?
8. **Commercial switches.** May a shop hide "Fit by Curtain"? And at what price do 3D, the AI-enhanced picture and "See it on me" become switchable (D17)?
